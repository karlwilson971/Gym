# PulseFit: Train smarter. Get stronger.

Your gym in your pocket. PulseFit is a fitness app to plan workouts, crush sets with a live rest timer, book gym classes and watch your strength progress climb.

## Everything in one app

- [Your daily dashboard](/dashboard): today's plan, weekly activity and goal rings at a glance.
- [Smart workout library](/library): moves filtered by muscle group, with sets, reps and difficulty dialed in.
- [Live workout mode](/workout): a big, bold rest timer and one-tap set tracking built for sweaty thumbs.
- [Progress you can see](/progress): strength curves and PR trophies that make every extra kilo feel earned.
- [Book classes in a tap](/classes): Yoga, HIIT, Spin and Boxing. See spots left and lock in your place.
- [Streaks that stick](/profile): daily streaks, badges and achievements that keep you coming back.
